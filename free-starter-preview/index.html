Free 06 · Questions

Clear before you start.

Are the first three modules really free?

Yes. The separate free product provides access to modules 1–3: 22 lessons. The signup step asks for your name and email, then sends you to the course.

What happens after I sign up?

You receive an email with the subject “Important: Your access to training”. Click the link, choose your password, and you are taken straight to your course. If you already have an account with us, log in with your usual password.

Who is the free path for?

AIQbiz is for business customers. The signup asks you to confirm that you are acting for your trade, business or self-employed professional activity.

Do I have to show my face?

No. The point of the course is an AI creator who is the face of your content, the way Ava and Ethan are. You stay behind the camera.

Do I need Higgsfield or ElevenLabs?

The source workflows use Higgsfield for images, video and Soul ID, and ElevenLabs for a designed creator voice. Provider accounts and their changing costs are separate from AIQbiz.

What does the Founding Edition add?

$97 once: all 38 BUILD lessons now, GROW on December 1, MONETIZE on December 15, 2026, and every update to these three stages. ADVANCED and AUTOMATE are in progress and are not part of the Founding Edition.
