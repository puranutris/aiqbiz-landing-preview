Free 05 · Signup questions

Before you sign up.

Who can sign up?

AIQbiz is for business customers only: freelancers, creators and small businesses who use it for their work. At signup you confirm that you are acting for your trade, business or self-employed professional activity, not predominantly for private purposes, and you accept the Terms for Business Customers.

What happens after I sign up?

You receive an email with the subject “Important: Your access to training”. Click the link, choose your password, and you are taken straight to your course. The email can take a few minutes; check your spam or promotions folder if you do not see it.

I already have an account. Do I sign up again?

Log in with your usual password. No new password is needed.

Do I have to pay anything?

No. The signup asks only for your name and email, no payment details. The Founding Edition is a separate, optional one-time purchase.

No email after 10 minutes?

Write to us at team@aiqbiz.com.
